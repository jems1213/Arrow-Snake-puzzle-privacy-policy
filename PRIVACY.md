# Privacy Policy — Arrow Snake Puzzle

**Last updated: 20 September 2026**

This policy explains what the Arrow Snake Puzzle mobile game
(`com.snackpuzzle.snack_puzzle_game`) does with your data. It is short because
the game does very little with it.

## We do not ask you for personal information

The game has no sign-up, no account and no login. We never ask for your name,
email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location or files, and the game
does not request any of those Android permissions.

## What stays on your device

Your progress and preferences are saved in the app's own local storage on your
device. This includes:

- Level progress, stars, coins, XP, streak and unlocked achievements
- Chosen theme and skins
- Settings such as sound, vibration, particles, screen shake, reduce motion and
  high-contrast HUD

We cannot see any of this. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else, it is not
backed up to a server by the game, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app
or use **Settings → Reset Progress**.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob**. They are
never forced: an ad only plays after you choose to watch one in exchange for a
reward such as an extra hint, a route reveal, an extra life or coins. There are
no banner or interstitial ads.

To serve those ads, Google's advertising SDK collects data directly from your
device. We do not receive it. Google acts as an independent controller of that
data, and it typically includes:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) | To select ads and limit how often the same ad repeats |
| IP address and coarse location derived from it | Region-appropriate ads and legal compliance |
| Device and app information (model, OS version, app version) | Ad compatibility and fraud prevention |
| Ad interaction events (views, completions, clicks) | Ad measurement and payment |

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your choices about ads

- You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in **Android Settings
  → Privacy → Ads**, which also lets you turn off ad personalisation.
- If you are in a region that requires a consent choice (such as the EEA, the UK
  or Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form the first time it runs.
  Where that applies, a **Privacy Choices** entry appears in the game's Settings
  screen so you can change your answer later.
- Declining personalised ads does not lock you out of anything: rewarded ads
  remain optional either way.

## No analytics or tracking by us

The game includes no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no social network
SDK and no advertising network other than AdMob. We do not build a profile of
you and we do not sell or share data, because we do not hold any.

## Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal
information through the game, contact us and we will act on it.

## Your rights

Because the game keeps no personal data on any server of ours, there is nothing
for us to export or erase on request. To remove everything the game holds, use
**Settings → Reset Progress** or uninstall the app. For data held by Google as
an ad provider, use the Google links above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a
new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's
store listing.
